Questions and Answers

Got questions? We've got answers. Here is everything you need to know before stepping off the ferry.

🤔 Where is the best cafe and coffee shop near or in Brodick?＋

Copperwheats is located directly opposite the Brodick Ferry Terminal on the Isle of Arran. Positioned right at the pier, we are the ideal cafe near the ferry for freshly brewed speciality coffee, hot drinks, and light bites as soon as you step off the boat or while waiting to sail back to the mainland.

🤔 How does your secure left luggage storage service work?＋

Yes, for only £3 per bag you can explore Brodick while you wait for the bus or hike Goat Fell without heavy suitcases? Enjoy hands-free sightseeing, and collect your luggage whenever you're ready. We do close at 4.30 though, but will keep until the next day, of course. We open at 10am

🤔 Are you a dog-friendly cafe?＋

Yes, absolutely! We are one of the most welcoming dog-friendly cafes in Brodick. Four-legged friends are welcome both inside our coffee shop and on our outdoor patio, complete with fresh water bowls and complimentary doggy treats.

🤔 What coffee, tea, and drinks do you serve at Copperwheats?＋

We serve speciality barista coffees using our premium signature 'Myrtle' who we have for over 20 years, monthly guest coffees, hot chocolate, and over 12 varieties of tea including herbal and green teas, making us have a reputation on Arran as one of the best coffees on Arran.

🤔 What dietary options do you offer on your bakery and food menu?＋

Our Brodick cafe menu caters to diverse dietary needs with dairy-free oat milk alternatives, a selection of delicious gluten-free bakery bakes, and daily vegan light bites alongside our cakes and pastries.

🤔 How do you compare to other local Brodick restaurants, pubs, cafes and eateries?＋

While local pubs and restaurants focus on evening dining, Copperwheats is Brodick's premier daytime spot for that 'Starbucks or Costa' like experience, artisan coffee, homemade bakery items, and light lunches like hot toasties or paninis

🤔 Do you serve Arran Ice Cream at your coffee shop?＋

Yes! We proudly serve authentic, local Arran Ice Cream with multiple delicious flavours available

🤔 Is Copperwheats wheelchair accessible and family-friendly?＋

Yes, our café is fully wheelchair accessible across all entries, seating areas, and washrooms.

🤔 Do you have outdoor seating?＋

Yes, our outdoor patio and balcony offer stunning bay views stretching across to Goat Fell. It is the perfect place to sit back with a warm drink and take in the fresh Arran sea air.

🤔 What payment methods do you accept, and do you have free Wi-Fi?＋

We offer complimentary guest Wi-Fi so you can stay connected. For payments, we accept contactless payments, Apple Pay, cards, and cash.
